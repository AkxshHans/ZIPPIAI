Questions, answered

More in the docs.

What is the Voice AI Companion?

An end-to-end AI companion that works as an AI operating system for your customer journey: pre-sales guidance, product selection and ordering, then order tracking, installation, operation, maintenance and troubleshooting.

Which channels does it work on?

Your website, WhatsApp, phone calls and email. It answers in real-time voice or text, depending on what each channel supports.

Does it replace our product manuals?

No. Manuals remain the reference; the companion makes that knowledge faster to reach by answering the exact question a customer asks.

Which languages does it speak?

Customers can use their native language. The companion supports 100+ languages.

What happens when it can't resolve an issue?

It connects the customer to live support or raises a ticket, and your agent gets an instant summary of what the customer has already tried.

Who is it for?

Enterprises, OEMs, manufacturers and brands that sell products needing guidance before and after purchase, from small brands on the Creator plan to enterprises needing custom models, SLAs and data residency.

Can field technicians use it?

Yes. Service engineers and installers get fast diagnostics on site and the right part identified.

Does it get better over time?

Yes. Product knowledge updates in real time, and the companion improves with each interaction. It also remembers each customer's context, preferences and history.

Do customers need to learn commands?

No. It's a natural conversation in voice or text, with no commands or technical terms needed, available 24x7.

How fast can we start?

Start a free trial with 100 credits for 7 days. No credit card required, with instant live setup.

How is it priced?

On monthly credits across four plans: Free trial, Creator, Growth and Enterprise. Contact sales for paid plans.
